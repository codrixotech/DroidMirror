DroidMirror shows your phone's screen on your Mac and lets you use it from there: click to tap, drag to swipe, scroll, and type with your Mac's keyboard. Incoming calls appear on your Mac, where you can answer or decline them.

This is the companion app for DroidMirror for Mac (free and open source). It's for phones where you'd rather not turn on Developer options.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Install DroidMirror on your Mac (github.com/codrixotech/DroidMirror).
2. In this app, allow control, then tap Start sharing.
3. Open DroidMirror on your Mac. Check that both show the same 6-digit code, and tap Allow.

After pairing, your Mac connects automatically whenever you start sharing.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your screen and input go only to your own paired Mac, over your local Wi-Fi network, encrypted.
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no servers. We never receive your data.
• Each Mac must be approved on the phone with a matching code.

PERMISSIONS
• Accessibility service: to perform the taps, swipes and typing you send from your Mac. DroidMirror reads only the text field you type into from the Mac.
• Screen sharing: Android asks each time you start sharing.
• Phone: to show incoming calls on your Mac and answer or end them from there.
• Notifications: for pairing requests.

Requires Android 12 or later and a Mac with Apple Silicon running macOS 14 or later, on the same Wi-Fi network.
